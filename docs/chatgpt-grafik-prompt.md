# Tumbi Køkken — billeder fra ChatGPT

1. Start en ny samtale i ChatGPT, og indsæt hele boksen «STYLE BIBLE» først. Vent på, at den svarer «OK».
2. Indsæt derefter én billedprompt ad gangen i rækkefølgen nedenfor, og begynd med de tre dyreark. Gem hvert billede som PNG med præcis det filnavn, der står i overskriften.
3. Læg alle billederne i Drev-mappen `Claude/Kogebog/billeder`. Claude henter dem derfra og lægger dem ind i appen.

## STYLE BIBLE (indsæt først)

```text
TUMBI KITCHEN - STYLE BIBLE
Read all of this first. It applies to every image in this conversation. Do not draw anything yet; just reply "OK".

ABOUT THE PROJECT
Tumbi Kitchen is a small cooking app for an iPad. A child (about 5-9 years old) and an adult cook together, one step per screen. The child can hardly read, so every picture must explain itself without words. The pictures must speak to both the child and the adult: warm, friendly and calm, a little grown-up and sensible - like a modern Scandinavian picture book or a well-designed pictogram. Not babyish: no oversized baby heads, no kawaii faces, no blush circles, no sparkles, no hearts.

FORMAT (every image, unless the prompt says otherwise)
- Square, 1024 x 1024 px, PNG.
- A real transparent background (alpha channel): no backdrop, no floor, no drawn checkerboard, no frame, no drop shadow.
- One clear subject, centred, with about 8 % empty margin on every side.
- NO TEXT anywhere: no letters, words, numbers, labels, logos, signatures or watermarks - also not on packages, jars, measuring cups or screens.

DRAWING STYLE
- Flat 2D vector illustration with flat colour fills. At most one slightly darker tint per colour for simple shading. No gradients, textures, noise, 3D, glossy highlights or photorealism.
- Thick, soft, rounded outlines in dark cocoa #3B2A20, the same line weight everywhere (about 14 px at 1024 px), with rounded ends and corners.
- Big, simple shapes and few small details. Every image must still be clear at 96 x 96 px.
- Food is simplified but easy to recognise and looks tasty (Danish home cooking).

PALETTE (use only these colours, plus one darker tint of each)
#3B2A20 dark cocoa - outlines and eyes
#FFF4E2 warm cream - light areas, plates, muzzles
#FFFFFF white - chef's hat, steam, eye highlights
#E2513B tomato red
#F08A3C fox orange
#F2C14E mustard yellow
#5BAA6A herb green
#3E8FB0 kitchen blue - water and motion arrows
#9A6A44 bear brown
#E8C79A dough beige - dough, bread, flour, wood
#8E9AA6 steel grey - pans, pots, knives, taps

SIGNS (the same in every image)
- Motion: at most two thick, curved kitchen-blue (#3E8FB0) arrows with rounded ends and a cocoa outline.
- Heat: two or three short wavy fox-orange lines under a pan or pot.
- Steam: two or three soft white curls with a cocoa outline.
- Adult task (knife or heat): the paw in the picture is Bo's big brown paw. In hot tasks it wears a tomato-red oven mitt.
- Child task: the paws are Rosa's small orange paws with cream palms.
- When a picture only needs paws, show just the paws and forearms coming in from the edge of the image. This is the only thing allowed to touch the edge.

THE THREE KITCHEN HELPERS (always exactly the same colours, proportions and clothes)
1. BO - the chef bear, the adult's helper. A grown-up brown bear standing upright, sturdy and calm, with kind eyes and a patient little smile. Fur bear brown #9A6A44; muzzle, belly patch and inner ears dough beige #E8C79A; round cocoa nose; round ears; short legs; big soft paws. His head is about one quarter of his body height (hat not counted). He wears a tall white chef's hat with three puffy bumps on top, and a kitchen-blue #3E8FB0 apron from chest to knees with one front pocket. No other clothes, no shoes.
2. ROSA - the little fox, the child's helper. A young fox standing upright, curious and eager. Fur fox orange #F08A3C; muzzle, chest, palms and tail tip warm cream #FFF4E2; pointed ears with cream inside; small cocoa nose; a big bushy tail. Her head is about one third of her body height. She wears a herb-green #5BAA6A apron from chest to mid-thigh and a tomato-red #E2513B neckerchief knotted at the side. She is about 60 % of Bo's height.
3. PIP - the little hedgehog, the second child's helper and the patient one who waits. A small, round hedgehog standing upright on short legs, gentle and calm. A soft, rounded cloud of short spines in bear brown #9A6A44 (with its darker tint) covers the back and the top of the head - never sharp or scary. Face, belly and paws dough beige #E8C79A; small round cocoa nose; small round ears. Pip wears a mustard-yellow #F2C14E apron and is about 40 % of Bo's height.
Faces for all three: simple dark cocoa oval eyes with one tiny white highlight, a small calm smile, no eyelashes, no blush, no teeth. Paws have four rounded fingers and no claws.

Reply only "OK" and wait for my next message.
```

## Tjek hvert billede

- Der må ikke være bogstaver eller tal i billedet.
- Baggrunden skal være rigtig gennemsigtig og ikke et tegnet ternet mønster. Undtagelsen er `app-ikon.png`. Kan ChatGPT ikke lave gennemsigtig baggrund, så gem billedet alligevel, og sig det til Claude.
- Dyret skal ligne sit ark (farver, tøj og størrelse).
- Filnavnet skal være præcis som overskriften.
- Er et billede forkert, så skriv på engelsk: «Same image, but … Keep everything else identical.» Begynder ChatGPT at glemme stilen, så indsæt stilbiblen igen.
